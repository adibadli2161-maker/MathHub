🎯 Our Learning Goals

In this adventure, pupils will learn to:

  • Identify the x-axis, y-axis and origin.
  • Read coordinates in the first quadrant.
  • Plot points on a coordinate grid.
  • Apply coordinates to locate objects.

Understanding the Coordinate Grid

The x-axis is the horizontal line.

The y-axis is the vertical line.

The origin is where both axes meet. Its coordinates are (0, 0).

Let's Explore the Coordinate Grid!

Learning Focus: Identify the x-axis, y-axis and origin (0, 0).
